## Context followed and corrections verified

Read the shared power-accounting contract, the relevant absolute-sum definitions, the statics/ledger/linkage derivations, compound ideal and finite reports, configuration declarations, and the relevant open-problem entries. In particular, the selected entries OP-EPI-05, OP-TRF-01, and OP-TRF-05 were read with their linked equations and scope. The linkage cycle entries and compound entries informed limits without being forced into hardware questions.

Corrections supported by independent derivation:

- Five zero lines, not five rays, in the unrestricted rate plane.
- Fixed orbital radius gives zero Coriolis work, not zero force in every rotating frame.
- Effective centrifugal energy is coordinate dependent, including spin second moments; it is not a new physical reservoir. Its explicit time derivative is not an extra physical shaft port.
- The locked ground store is positive and the carrier effective store negative; both endpoint differences vanish.
- Only a specific sun mesh side has the positive work quoted generically for “each mesh side”; ring and opposite sides are explicitly retained.
- The mass coefficient in m=mu r^2 includes pi and is not literally an areal density.
- The torque-sum equation needs compatible geometry; the physical angular-momentum law does not fail off a fictitious incompatible assembly.
- Misphased and ground-phased Hooke finite-interval counts contain periodic endpoint corrections. Mean counts alone are insufficient.
- A negative output torque can drive a negatively rotating output; it is not automatically a passive brake.
- The no-frame-neutral conclusion concerns one unchanged rotor observed against two stators, not every possible relative-measurement device.
- R=1 equates the coaxial member rates; it does not necessarily stop the carrier. Zero-throughput ratios remain undefined.
- The cited MIT notes3 PDF is Polyphase Networks (Kirtley, 2001), not a verified transformer reference; excluded from the article.
